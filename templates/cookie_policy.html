What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

How We Use Cookies

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Cookies Required for basic site functionality (login, security) Session / Persistent
Preference Cookies Remember your settings and preferences 1 year
Analytics Cookies Understand how users interact with our platform 2 years
Session Cookies Keep you logged in during your visit Session

Specific Cookies We Use

Cookie Name Provider Purpose Expiry
sessionid MarketLens Maintains user session Session
csrftoken MarketLens CSRF protection 1 year
theme MarketLens Remembers dark/light mode preference 1 year
comparison_products MarketLens Stores temporary comparison list Session
recently_viewed MarketLens Tracks recently viewed products 30 days

Third-Party Cookies

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Analyzes site usage
  • Bootstrap CDN: Delivers CSS and JavaScript
  • Google Fonts: Provides typography

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

Browser Instructions
  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Site permissions → Cookies and site data
Note: Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

Cookie Consent

When you first visit MarketLens, you'll see a cookie banner. By continuing to use our site, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.
