Vendor Guidelines

Best practices and rules for selling on MarketLens

Code of Conduct

  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date pricing information
  • Respond to customer inquiries promptly and professionally
  • Honor the prices listed on the platform
  • Maintain product availability and quality standards
  • Respect customer privacy and data protection

Prohibited Practices

  • Manipulating prices or engaging in price fixing
  • Listing counterfeit or unauthorized products
  • Sharing competitor pricing information
  • Using fake reviews or ratings
  • Harassing or discriminating against customers

Account Management

KYC Requirements

All vendors must complete KYC verification with one of the following:

  • National Identification Number (NIN)
  • Bank Verification Number (BVN)
  • Government Issued ID Card
Business Information

Keep your business information up to date:

  • Business name and description
  • Market location and stall number
  • Contact information
  • Operating hours

Pricing Guidelines

Remember: Competitors cannot see your prices. This protects your pricing strategy.
Price Updates
  • Update prices at least weekly to maintain accuracy
  • Use bulk update tools for multiple products
  • Set price alerts for market changes
Best Practices
  • Price competitively to attract more customers
  • Consider bundle pricing for multiple items
  • Update prices during market fluctuations

Product Listing Standards

Product Information
  • Use clear, accurate product names
  • Specify the correct unit (kg, basket, piece, etc.)
  • Add product descriptions for clarity
  • Upload high-quality product images when possible
Categories
  • Select the most appropriate category for your products
  • Request new categories if needed (admin will review)
  • Don't list products in incorrect categories
