KYC Verification Terms

Please read these terms carefully before submitting your KYC documents

Last Updated: March 7, 2025

1. Purpose of KYC Verification

Why we need your information:

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification helps us maintain marketplace integrity, prevent fraud, and build trust between vendors and shoppers. By verifying your identity, you're showing customers that you're a legitimate business owner.

  • Verify your identity - Confirm you are a real person and legitimate business owner
  • Prevent fraudulent activities - Protect shoppers from scams and fake vendors
  • Build trust - Display a verified badge on your vendor profile
  • Comply with regulations - Meet legal requirements for online marketplaces

2. Accepted Documents

We accept the following government-issued identification documents:

National Identification Number (NIN)

Nigerian National ID Card

Bank Verification Number (BVN)

Central Bank of Nigeria

Government Issued ID

Driver's License, Voter's Card, International Passport

Note: All documents must be valid and not expired. We only accept clear, legible copies where all information is visible.

3. Document Requirements

File Format
JPG, PNG, PDF
Maximum File Size
5MB per document
Image Quality
Clear, well-lit, no blur
Document Condition
Valid, not expired
  • All four corners of the document must be visible
  • No glare or reflections that obscure important information
  • Text must be readable - name, document number, and expiration date
  • No alterations or edits to the document image
