Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last Updated: {{ last_updated }}

At MarketLens, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information
  • Name and contact information (email, phone number)
  • Username and password
  • Profile information and preferences
KYC Information (Vendors Only)
  • Government-issued ID details (NIN, BVN, or ID card)
  • Business name and market location
  • Document images for verification
Usage Information
  • Products viewed and searched
  • Price alerts and comparisons
  • Favorites and saved items
  • IP address and device information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Verify vendor identities and prevent fraud
  • Send price alerts and notifications
  • Improve and personalize user experience
  • Communicate with you about your account
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With vendors as necessary for platform functionality
  • With service providers who assist in operating our platform
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • With your consent
Note: Vendor KYC documents are only visible to authorized admin staff.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure data storage and transmission

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Export your data

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze platform usage
  • Improve user experience

You can control cookies through your browser settings.
