Price Update Policy

How prices are managed on MarketLens

Overview

MarketLens is committed to providing accurate, real-time pricing information to help consumers make informed decisions. This policy outlines how prices are updated, verified, and maintained on our platform.

For Vendors

Price Updates
  • Vendors can update their prices at any time through their dashboard
  • Price changes take effect immediately upon saving
  • Bulk price updates are available for multiple products
  • Price history is maintained for transparency
Update Frequency

We recommend vendors update their prices:

  • Daily for fresh produce and perishable items
  • Weekly for stable commodities
  • Immediately when market prices change significantly
Note: Outdated prices (not updated for 30+ days) may be flagged as "stale" and could affect your vendor ranking.

For Consumers

Price Accuracy
  • Prices shown are as provided by verified vendors
  • Last updated timestamp indicates when price was last confirmed
  • We encourage reporting outdated or incorrect prices
Price Alerts
  • Set target prices for products you're interested in
  • Receive notifications when prices drop to your target
  • Choose instant, daily, or weekly alert frequency

Price Verification

How We Verify Prices
  • Automated checks for unusual price fluctuations
  • Regular sampling and spot checks by our team
  • User reports and feedback system
  • Cross-referencing with market averages
Reporting Incorrect Prices

If you notice an incorrect price:

  1. Click the "Report" button on the product page
  2. Select the issue type (wrong price, outdated, etc.)
  3. Provide the correct price if known
  4. Our team will investigate within 24 hours

Price History

MarketLens maintains price history for all products to help users:

  • Track price trends over time
  • Identify seasonal price patterns
  • Make informed purchasing decisions
  • Verify fair pricing

Price history is visible on each product's detail page and can be filtered by time periods (week, month, year).

Policy Enforcement

Vendors who consistently provide inaccurate pricing may face:

  • Warning notifications
  • Temporary suspension of price update privileges
  • Reduced visibility in search results
  • Account suspension for repeated violations
